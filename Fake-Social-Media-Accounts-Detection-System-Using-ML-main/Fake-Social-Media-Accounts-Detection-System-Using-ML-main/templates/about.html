HOW IT WORKS

Learn from patterns. Review the evidence.

The development flow

  1. Load labeled account statistics from data.csv.
  2. Validate features and labels, then remove exact duplicate rows.
  3. Split unique accounts into 70% training and 30% test sets, stratified by class.
  4. Train Random Forest, Decision Tree, and scaled Logistic Regression.
  5. Evaluate models on held-out accounts and save models with their measured metrics.
  6. Validate entered or uploaded account statistics and apply the Random Forest.
View evaluation results →

What the model sees

Profile picture presence, digit ratios in username and full name, full-name word count, name/username equality, bio length, external link presence, privacy, posts, followers and following.

What the result means

“Likely genuine” and “Likely fake” are model classifications. Confidence is an uncalibrated estimate; it is not proof of account authenticity.

The dataset is small, and its collection and labeling sources are not documented. Testing on this dataset does not establish performance on other social platforms.
